Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

This App ("Postiz for OpenShorts") is a personal, self-hosted social media publishing tool operated by Udo Onyekwere (udo.donyekwere@gmail.com) on private infrastructure (Railway). It is not a public service and does not have other users.

What is processed

  • Media you submit: video clips and captions you choose to publish are transferred to and stored on the App's private server long enough to upload them to the social platforms you connect.
  • Platform credentials: when you authorise the App through TikTok Login Kit, the App stores OAuth access/refresh tokens for your account, used only to publish content on your behalf. TikTok's user info (display name, avatar, account identifier) is read for the connection.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no third-party trackers are present in the App.

Who it is shared with

Only with the platforms you connect (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube), as required for publishing via their official APIs. No data is sold or shared with anyone else.

Storage and deletion

Data lives on the operator's private Railway deployment. You may revoke the App's access at any time in your TikTok settings (Security > Devices > Manage apps / Apps and websites), which stops token use immediately; the operator can delete stored media and tokens on request at udo.donyekwere@gmail.com.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and knowingly collects no data from them.
